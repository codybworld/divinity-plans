Business overview

WHAT WE DO // WHY IT EXISTS

Divinity Creative Co. is an independent web design and creative studio serving businesses in the Buffalo, NY area and remotely. We make an effective online presence more approachable by combining custom website design with affordable monthly maintenance and direct support.

Instead of leaving you with a website you don't know how to update, we build it, launch it, and remain available for agreed routine changes. You focus on customers; we take care of the technical details.

Core website services

  • Small-business website design and development
  • Hosting setup and secure deployment
  • Domain connection and basic search setup
  • Ongoing maintenance and routine updates
  • Website redesigns and visual refreshes

Related creative services

  • Logos and visual identities
  • Social media and digital advertisements
  • Flyers, apparel graphics, and marketing assets
  • Custom illustrations and section icons
  • Advanced custom development by quote
Our guiding idea: We build it. We host it. We maintain it. You focus on your business.

What is a small content update?

Examples: changing opening hours, correcting a phone number, replacing an existing photo, updating a few sentences, adjusting listed services or prices, and switching an existing social link. Two requests are included per subscription month; unused requests don't roll over.

A new page, major redesign, advanced integration, brand-new feature, full copy rewrite, or bulk content replacement is quoted separately.

Hosting & technology

We design and maintain websites using professional development tools rather than locking every client into the same website builder. Our typical process uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Visual Studio Code, GitHub, and a suitable hosting provider.

01 — DEVELOP

Reusable foundations customized to each business, with responsive layouts, optimized assets, and accessible interactions.

02 — DEPLOY

Changes are saved with version history and published to the appropriate hosting environment after testing.

03 — MAINTAIN

We handle agreed routine edits, website fixes, and deployment-related support.

Hosting approach

Simple informational sites may be hosted on Cloudflare Pages or a comparable static hosting service. More demanding projects may require paid hosting such as DigitalOcean or another provider. We choose based on actual site needs and platform limits—not because every customer needs their own virtual server.

Domains and email

We can help connect your preferred business domain. You should normally register it in your own name and retain control of its registrar account. The cost of the domain is separate; business email inbox hosting is also separate unless included in an individual quote.

Free hosting tiers can have usage, feature, and commercial-policy limits. We confirm eligibility and any additional charges before selecting a production platform.

  • Fixes to errors in the agreed existing site

  • Basic service and contact information edits
  • Replacing supplied images in existing sections

↗ Separately quoted work

  • New pages or major structural changes
  • Fresh branding, logos, or extensive copywriting
  • Online store and scheduling integrations
  • Custom backends, databases, and member areas
  • Large batches of edits and content migration

How support timing works

We aim to acknowledge requests within one business day and complete routine, in-scope updates within one to two business days after receiving the needed materials. These are targets, not guaranteed turnaround times. More involved changes, weekends, third-party failures, or outages may take longer.

We'll explain the scope and price of larger work before proceeding. Each small update should be a clearly defined change to existing content; multiple unrelated edits or a redesign are not automatically one request.

Ownership, access & cancellation

Clarity about ownership matters. You should not have to guess who controls your domain, what happens when a subscription ends, or whether you can take your content elsewhere.

What belongs to you

  • Your domain: registered in your own name/account whenever practical; domain costs paid separately.
  • Your supplied material: you keep ownership of the business text, photography, logos, and other content you provide (subject to third-party rights).
  • Website files/design rights: exact transfer or license rights for custom code, preexisting templates, stock assets, and custom designs are specified in the written agreement.

Access while subscribed

We'll provide the site URL and documented support contact process. Direct editing credentials are included only where a self-editing system was explicitly agreed and configured. Full access to hosting/source systems depends on the ownership and service arrangement.

Ending or transferring service

You may request an export or transfer according to the agreement. We will explain available files, the process for changing hosting, any third-party licenses, and the timing of service termination. The agreement will make any remaining charges, cancellation deadlines, and migration assistance fees clear in advance.

The information on this page describes our proposed service model and is not itself a binding offer, contract, or guarantee. Your written project quote and signed service agreement govern final pricing, deliverables, ownership, and cancellation rights.

Frequently asked questions

Is the $150 a one-time charge?

$100 is the one-time setup fee. The other $50 pays for your first subscription month. The standard $50 recurring fee begins with the next monthly billing cycle.

Does my business get its own domain name?

Yes, you can use a custom domain. Domain registration and renewals are billed separately. We can help connect it to your website, and we recommend keeping the domain registered in your name.

Can I edit the site myself?

The standard package is managed by us: you send updates and we make the in-scope edits. A separate content dashboard can be quoted for compatible websites if you'd like more direct control.

What if I need more than five pages?

We can scope and quote additional pages. Complex sites, databases, shopping carts, and specialized integrations aren't included in the standard $150 launch offer.

What happens if I cancel?

The proposed offer has a six-month initial term. Specific cancellation and transfer rules are documented before payment. Your domain and supplied content remain yours; code/design rights depend on the written agreement.

Will my site rank #1 on Google?

No one can responsibly guarantee that. We include basic on-page search setup, but rankings depend on many factors. Ongoing SEO campaigns and marketing services are separate.

What if I already have a website?

We can discuss rebuilding or updating it. The scope depends on your current platform, content, access, and the features you need.

Do you offer business logos or other graphics?

Yes. Logo work, visual identity, social graphics, and other creative services are available by separate quote.
